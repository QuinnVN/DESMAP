# Weight the Sales score toward an accepted, satisfying remedy

Under sales-rubric-v3.2 each of ten rubric components earned 10 points. An experienced salesperson who settled the complaint in two turns scored 50: the dialogue ends once concerns 1–3 are resolved, so the trust challenge never appears, and three overlapping investigation components each counted separately. The assessment is meant to estimate fit for sales work, where a satisfied customer matters most.

sales-rubric-v3.3 keeps the two 0–50 criteria so result consumers keep their scale, and changes what fills them:

- apologyAndPolicyRemedy: acknowledgment 5, policy-compliant exchange 20, suitable remedy (lightweight) 15, trial 10.
- adaptabilityAndDeescalation: any investigation question (open, use or fit) 10, responsibility 10, customer satisfaction 15, efficiency 15.

Satisfaction and efficiency apply only when Lan accepts a policy-compliant remedy. Satisfaction is 15 when the dialogue ends with objectives_completed and the latest remedy matches Lan's real situation, and 10 when she accepts with reservations: an exchange_accepted ending, or a remedy built on a fit problem she never had. Efficiency is 15 up to five turns, then 10 at six, 5 at seven and 0 at eight; clarification turns do not count. Most participants are students, so a careful five-turn conversation keeps the full score. Cause and explanation still resolve concerns and set the evidence flags but earn no points, so a wrong but satisfying guess is not penalized twice.

The mismatch comes from a new Jev label, remedyContradictsFit, in question set sales-jev-v7. Customer wording still creates no evidence. An uncertain mismatch never holds the turn for clarification and costs nothing; Luna verifies it last while concern 3 is open. A mismatch also prevents restored trust, so Lan's ending tone carries her reservations.

## Consequences

Quick accepted remedies score higher and unresolved dialogues lower than under v3.2. A complete conversation can still reach 100 in five turns. The remedyContradictsFit threshold uses the generic 0.8/0.2 rule and has not been calibrated against reviewed labels. Stored ledgers lack the label, so offline rescoring of older sessions treats every remedy as matching.
